About us · Our impact

Play with purpose

At Grås Games, we believe that the joy of play should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or circumstances.

01

Purposeful play

As a social enterprise, we strive to use the medium of tabletop gaming to bring people together and help foster social connections. We hope to create positive experiences for all communities, with a particular focus on three groups.

To engage with these communities, we actively organise events such as community gaming sessions, game introductions, showcases, and other activities. We also help facilitate and host externally run sessions.

01

Low-income individuals

We strongly believe that financial circumstances should not be a barrier to experiencing the joys of community play.

That’s why we host complimentary gaming sessions at our cafe-space during our weekly Community Nights. During these nights, participants are also welcome to contribute towards the Fund, sponsoring opportunities for others in the community.

Eligible individuals can also apply for Complimentary or Subsidised Clüb Passes, which give unlimited access to the game library during normal operations. These passes are indistinguishable from the standard paid passes, providing anonymity and avoiding any stigma.

02

Under-served and vulnerable groups, such as the elderly or socially isolated

Having experienced it firsthand, our Founder understands how pervasive loneliness and social isolation are in modern society.

While we may intuitively associate these issues only with elderly individuals living alone, or seniors with limited social interaction, we cannot forget that younger generations are equally vulnerable: people who are socially isolated or disconnected from their communities, who have difficulty making friends, or who are dealing with major life changes.

During our hosted community sessions, we make an extra effort to match players and foster intergenerational ties, where younger and older participants can play and interact together as equals, regardless of age or background.

We also carefully consider how best to pair individuals with different levels of social openness, giving introverted or quiet players a chance to express themselves while safely staying within their comfort zones.

All sessions at our cafe come with the option to book either a Private or a Community Table. Community Tables are open for other players to join, great for individuals or groups who would like to expand their social circle.

03

The arts and design community

Beyond engaging vulnerable communities, we also recognise the importance of supporting the wider ecosystem of tabletop gaming, and hope to promote greater visibility and opportunities for artists, designers, and independent creators.

Behind every game are illustrators, graphic designers, product designers, writers, developers and manufacturers, yet independent creatives often have limited resources, exposure and opportunities.

We hope to serve as a platform for independent artists, designers, and other creatives to share their work, gain exposure, and connect with a wider audience.

Growing together

On top of these subsidised sessions and community programmes, we are constantly on the lookout for partnerships with social service and community organisations alike. We hope these efforts will help provide gaming opportunities for individuals who may otherwise find it difficult to participate.

We recognise that there is still much more we can do. As we grow, we aspire to develop even more meaningful opportunities for everyone to participate in, contribute to, and bring about meaningful change. Sometimes, something as simple as bringing people together around a table can ignite the most meaningful of impacts.

If you would like to volunteer, please reach out to us at gras.games.sg+volunteer@gmail.com.

Sustainable play

We recognise that the tabletop gaming industry, for all the joy and creativity it brings, also has an environmental footprint. From manufacturing and packaging to materials and logistics, there are challenges that we cannot simply overlook.

While we understand that sustainability is an ongoing journey, we believe that small, conscious decisions can collectively make a difference. We strive to encourage more environmentally responsible practices wherever possible, from packaging and material choices to the way we operate and deliver our services.

We want to contribute to a tabletop gaming community that not only grows, but grows responsibly.

  • Grås Games is dedicated to using 100% upcycled and reused materials in packaging for shipping and freight, and reusable bags for in-store pickups.
  • We pledge to reduce unnecessary packaging materials, and always use upcycled materials for shipping and mailing wherever possible.
  • In production, we are committed to reducing and minimising waste from prototypes and fabrication, recycling or reusing them. We are also exploring material alternatives that are sustainable and responsibly sourced.
  • We invite customers to reuse or donate games, and we’ll be happy to help organise board game exchanges, markets, or auctions that help redistribute pre-loved games.
