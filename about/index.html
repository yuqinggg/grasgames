About us

At Grås Games, we believe that tabletop games are so much more than just entertainment. They are experiences that bring people together, spark creativity, encourage discovery, and create meaningful connections.

What began as a personal endeavour to help fellow enthusiasts discover and source hard-to-find board games has since grown into something much bigger. Today, Grås Games is a tabletop gaming brand and social enterprise built around our love for games, creativity, community, and the people who make it all possible.

Our goal is simple: to make the wonderful world of tabletop gaming more accessible, inclusive, and meaningful to everyone.

01

Our story

Since
2018

Our story starts in 2018, as an individual exploratory initiative, long before Grås Games was officially established.

Through online communities and forums, we helped fellow gaming enthusiasts from all around the world source and ship games, particularly titles from Asian publishers that were not readily available to international audiences.

We felt that there were so many wonderful games being created, especially by smaller and independent publishers, that simply weren’t reaching the wider tabletop gaming community. Even for those who knew about these titles, getting their hands on a copy was often a challenge.

What started as a way to bridge that gap gradually developed into a personalised sourcing service, followed by small-volume imports and distribution to Singapore and beyond.

Over time, our journey expanded beyond simply bringing games to people. We wanted to create opportunities for people to discover, experience, and connect through them.

Today, Grås Games has grown to encompass curated gaming spaces, community events, creative services, and a growing network of publishers, designers, artists, and fellow enthusiasts, and we’re still growing.

02

Beyond the board

Unlike a typical retailer, distributor, or board game cafe, we aspire to be a service-focused platform that connects different parts of the tabletop gaming community and ecosystem.

Our Design-to-Table Approach shines a well-deserved spotlight on interesting and unique titles by both established and independent publishers.

This way, not only are we able to help gamers discover a new side to games, we are also able to focus on the hard work behind production, and feature the designers, creators, studios, publishers, and manufacturers behind the game.

03

Our impact

We organise community gaming sessions, game introductions, and showcases, and strive to make our spaces and activities more accessible. This includes providing complimentary gaming opportunities for individuals who may otherwise find it difficult to participate due to financial constraints.

We also hope to serve as a platform for independent artists, designers, and other creatives to share their work, gain exposure, and connect with a wider audience.

To us, social impact isn’t something separate from what we do. It is an integral part of why we do it.

How we play with purpose Community Nights, Clüb Passes, matching players across generations, and how we keep our footprint small.

04

What’s next?

There is still so much we hope to do.

We want to introduce more people to the diversity of tabletop gaming, build stronger connections with publishers and creators, expand our creative services, and make it easier for enthusiasts to discover and access games through our upcoming online platform.

We also hope to continue growing our community initiatives, creating more opportunities for people to connect, participate, and experience the joy of play.

Ultimately, we want Grås Games to be a bridge between different communities: publishers and players, creators and communities, and people from all walks of life.
